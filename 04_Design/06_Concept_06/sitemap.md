Conversation-centered navigation: Talk, My Notes, Options, Plan, Share.

```text

├── Personal roadmap

├── Share summary
```

## Responsive navigation

- Desktop: Compact top navigation that gives the conversation most of the canvas.
- Mobile: Large Talk button with quiet secondary tabs for Notes and Plan.
- Focus mode: Interview and assessment views suppress nonessential navigation but retain Exit, Save, Help, and Privacy.
