# Nara — เพื่อนแนะแนวดิจิทัล

## Concept snapshot

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Concept name** | Nara / เพื่อนแนะแนวดิจิทัล |
| **One-sentence description** | A calm digital counselor provides a confidential place to untangle pressure, choices, and practical next steps. |
| **Main idea** | A trustworthy guidance companion that extends—not replaces—the school counselor. |
| **Target user group** | Thai students in ม.3–ม.6 who feel pressured by family, grades, or uncertain choices. |
| **Main user problem** | Students rarely receive enough private, non-judgmental counseling time. |
| **Unique value proposition** | A private conversation that prepares students for better human guidance conversations. |
| **Emotional experience** | Held, calmer, respected, and ready to talk with a trusted adult. |
| **Visual direction** | Soft sage interiors, natural materials, curved seating, and an original pebble-shaped AI presence. |
| **Development difficulty** | Medium–High |
| **Recommended technology** | Next.js, Web Speech optional layer, streaming LLM, safety classifier, encrypted notes, RBAC share packets. |

## Main features

- Private chat/voice
- Reflective summaries
- Pressure mapping
- Human counselor handoff
- Consent-based sharing
- Route options
- Weekly plan

## Advantages

- Strong emotional trust
- Clear ecosystem role with teachers
- Good for sensitive decision contexts

## Disadvantages

- Needs robust safety policy and escalation
- AI anthropomorphism must be carefully limited

## Why this fits Future Me

It operationalizes Motivational Interviewing, reflective listening, privacy boundaries, and counselor collaboration.

## Product positioning

**Category:** Digital guidance counselor  
**Promise:** A private conversation that prepares students for better human guidance conversations.  
**Primary CTA:** คุยกับนารา  
**Voice:** Calm, empathetic, boundaried, and never clinical or authoritative.

## Distinct interaction model

Voice-or-text counseling session with reflective summaries and consent checkpoints.

### Navigation structure

Conversation-centered navigation: Talk, My Notes, Options, Plan, Share.

- Desktop: Compact top navigation that gives the conversation most of the canvas.
- Mobile: Large Talk button with quiet secondary tabs for Notes and Plan.

### Core layouts

- **Homepage:** Quiet editorial hero: broad breathing room, counselor-room visual, trust and privacy assurances.
- **AI interview:** A spacious counseling room UI with an optional voice waveform and private notes drawer.
- **Dashboard:** Wellbeing-aware home with check-in, unresolved decisions, next conversation, and share packet.
- **Results:** A counselor’s summary: what we heard, possible routes, what needs checking, and who can help.
- **Roadmap:** A gentle weekly plan with a human support contact attached to difficult steps.

## Design guardrails

- Present routes as hypotheses for educational exploration, never guaranteed predictions.
- Keep private chat content separate from any parent/teacher summary.
- Keep official admissions facts, source dates, and assumptions distinguishable.
- Offer a list alternative to any graph, map, or highly visual interaction.
- Make “ยังไม่รู้” and “แก้ไขสิ่งที่ AI เข้าใจ” first-class actions.
