# Tomorrow Stories — เรื่องเล่าของวันพรุ่งนี้

## Concept snapshot

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Concept name** | Tomorrow Stories / เรื่องเล่าของวันพรุ่งนี้ |
| **One-sentence description** | Students enter short branching stories that reveal preferences through choices, consequences, and reflection. |
| **Main idea** | An interactive narrative anthology that lets students rehearse unfamiliar work contexts. |
| **Target user group** | Creative Thai students in ม.2–ม.6 who engage with stories more than dashboards. |
| **Main user problem** | Career descriptions are abstract and fail to show what everyday work can feel like. |
| **Unique value proposition** | Experience a five-minute work story, then unpack what your choices reveal. |
| **Emotional experience** | Immersed, curious, expressive, and safely challenged. |
| **Visual direction** | Graphic-novel panels, bold ink, risograph texture, chapter cards, and cinematic branching doors. |
| **Development difficulty** | High |
| **Recommended technology** | Next.js narrative state machine, headless CMS, authored decision trees, FastAPI evidence extraction, analytics. |

## Main features

- Branching career stories
- Choice replay
- Socratic debrief
- Evidence clues
- Perspective switch
- Route chapters
- Story accessibility controls

## Advantages

- Highly memorable
- Makes roles concrete
- Supports scenario-based evidence

## Disadvantages

- Expensive narrative content
- May bias users toward dramatized scenarios

## Why this fits Future Me

It combines Scenario Missions with STAR reflection and avoids asking students to imagine careers from labels alone.

## Product positioning

**Category:** Story-based career adventure  
**Promise:** Experience a five-minute work story, then unpack what your choices reveal.  
**Primary CTA:** เปิดเรื่องแรก  
**Voice:** Vivid, concise, curious, and reflective after each choice.

## Distinct interaction model

Branching narrative choices followed by Socratic debrief and a replay-from-another-perspective option.

### Navigation structure

Chapter navigation: Library, Current Story, Clues, Endings, My Next Chapter.

- Desktop: Editorial masthead with chapter index and reading progress.
- Mobile: Swipeable chapter deck with tap zones and persistent accessibility controls.

### Core layouts

- **Homepage:** Magazine-cover hero with strong type, generated doorway panorama, and featured story cards.
- **AI interview:** Questions appear between story beats as a director’s debrief rather than a chat window.
- **Dashboard:** A bookshelf of completed stories, discovered clues, alternate endings, and next recommended scene.
- **Results:** A narrative dossier linking choices to behavior evidence and three possible next chapters.
- **Roadmap:** A chapter arc where each milestone is a scene, project, or real conversation.

## Design guardrails

- Present routes as hypotheses for educational exploration, never guaranteed predictions.
- Keep private chat content separate from any parent/teacher summary.
- Keep official admissions facts, source dates, and assumptions distinguishable.
- Offer a list alternative to any graph, map, or highly visual interaction.
- Make “ยังไม่รู้” and “แก้ไขสิ่งที่ AI เข้าใจ” first-class actions.
