# Pulse — ฟีดทดลองอนาคต

## Concept snapshot

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Concept name** | Pulse / ฟีดทดลองอนาคต |
| **One-sentence description** | A private, visual discovery feed turns small experiments into shareable learning—not popularity. |
| **Main idea** | A mobile-first discovery feed for students accustomed to visual stories and short interactions. |
| **Target user group** | Thai Gen-Z students in ม.2–university year 1 who prefer short, visual, social formats. |
| **Main user problem** | Long assessments lose attention, while public social platforms reward popularity rather than reflection. |
| **Unique value proposition** | Swipe through private mini-experiments, react with how it felt, and build a visual evidence reel. |
| **Emotional experience** | Expressive, current, included, and energized. |
| **Visual direction** | Hot coral and ultraviolet gradients, editorial collage, story frames, sticker shapes, and bold type. |
| **Development difficulty** | Medium–High |
| **Recommended technology** | Next.js/PWA, touch gesture layer, media upload service, content moderation, FastAPI, object storage, privacy controls. |

## Main features

- Private discovery feed
- Swipe experiments
- Energy/effort reactions
- Evidence reel
- Visual route carousel
- Friend co-op opt-in
- Strong sharing controls

## Advantages

- Highest Gen-Z appeal
- Excellent mobile demo
- Supports frequent re-engagement

## Disadvantages

- Risk of superficiality and social comparison
- Needs rigorous privacy and anti-ranking design

## Why this fits Future Me

It translates short Scenario Missions and long-term progress tracking into a familiar mobile grammar while removing popularity metrics.

## Product positioning

**Category:** Gen-Z social and visual experience  
**Promise:** Swipe through private mini-experiments, react with how it felt, and build a visual evidence reel.  
**Primary CTA:** ปัดดูภารกิจ  
**Voice:** Short, vivid, inclusive, and transparent about privacy.

## Distinct interaction model

Swipe experiment cards, hold to preview, submit a quick artifact, and react with effort/energy—not likes.

### Navigation structure

Mobile feed navigation: Discover, Try, Reel, Paths, Me.

- Desktop: A three-column social workspace with feed, active experiment, and private reel.
- Mobile: Five-item bottom dock with a prominent Try action.

### Core layouts

- **Homepage:** High-energy collage hero with a phone-like experiment deck and safety-first private-feed promise.
- **AI interview:** AI prompts appear as short visual story cards with voice or text replies.
- **Dashboard:** A private feed of missions, evidence reel, saved paths, and progress moments.
- **Results:** A visual route carousel backed by expandable evidence captions and no public rank.
- **Roadmap:** A vertical story reel of upcoming experiments and milestones.

## Design guardrails

- Present routes as hypotheses for educational exploration, never guaranteed predictions.
- Keep private chat content separate from any parent/teacher summary.
- Keep official admissions facts, source dates, and assumptions distinguishable.
- Offer a list alternative to any graph, map, or highly visual interaction.
- Make “ยังไม่รู้” and “แก้ไขสิ่งที่ AI เข้าใจ” first-class actions.
