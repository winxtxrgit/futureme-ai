# Compass Coach — เข็มทิศอนาคต

## Concept snapshot

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Concept name** | Compass Coach / เข็มทิศอนาคต |
| **One-sentence description** | A warm AI coach turns reflection and small experiments into three understandable next-step routes. |
| **Main idea** | A reassuring personal career coach for students who need a calm first step. |
| **Target user group** | Thai students in ม.3–ม.6 who feel overwhelmed by choices and want conversational guidance. |
| **Main user problem** | Career information is fragmented, advice feels generic, and students do not know where to begin. |
| **Unique value proposition** | One calm conversation becomes three evidence-backed routes and one achievable mission. |
| **Emotional experience** | Reassured, seen, capable, and gently motivated. |
| **Visual direction** | Warm editorial paper-cut landscapes, compass metaphors, rounded cards, and generous calm space. |
| **Development difficulty** | Medium |
| **Recommended technology** | Semantic HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype; production Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Qdrant, rule engine, and Thai-capable LLM. |

## Main features

- Socratic AI coach
- STAR evidence notebook
- RIASEC interest signals
- Scenario missions
- Three-route comparison
- 30-day roadmap
- Consent-based sharing

## Advantages

- Lowest onboarding anxiety
- Easy to explain in a demo
- Strong trust and guidance tone

## Disadvantages

- Less novel than game or story concepts
- Coach quality depends heavily on dialogue design

## Why this fits Future Me

It expresses the knowledge base’s Socratic, motivational, multi-route approach in the clearest mainstream product form.

## Product positioning

**Category:** AI career discovery coach  
**Promise:** One calm conversation becomes three evidence-backed routes and one achievable mission.  
**Primary CTA:** เริ่มค้นหาเส้นทาง  
**Voice:** Warm, brief, reflective, never directive.

## Distinct interaction model

Coach-led conversation with progressive disclosure and a single next-best action.

### Navigation structure

Top navigation organized around Discover, My Paths, Roadmap, and Saved.

- Desktop: Wide top bar with persistent Start discovery CTA and visible trust note.
- Mobile: Compact header plus four-item bottom navigation.

### Core layouts

- **Homepage:** Editorial split hero: copy left, generated path illustration right, followed by a three-step discovery strip.
- **AI interview:** A side-by-side coach conversation and live evidence notebook.
- **Dashboard:** Action-first dashboard led by Today’s next step, route health, saved choices, and counselor-share control.
- **Results:** Three route cards—Balanced, Growth, Practical—anchored by evidence and open questions.
- **Roadmap:** A vertical step path with expandable milestones and optional branches.

## Design guardrails

- Present routes as hypotheses for educational exploration, never guaranteed predictions.
- Keep private chat content separate from any parent/teacher summary.
- Keep official admissions facts, source dates, and assumptions distinguishable.
- Offer a list alternative to any graph, map, or highly visual interaction.
- Make “ยังไม่รู้” and “แก้ไขสิ่งที่ AI เข้าใจ” first-class actions.
