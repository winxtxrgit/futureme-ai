# Aurora Page Structure

The product uses Aurora glass-bento presentation, but every layout remains task-first, Thai-first, mobile-responsive, and understandable without decorative effects.

| Page | Page objective | Main content | Important components | Main CTA | User interaction | Mobile layout considerations | Desktop layout considerations | Accessibility considerations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Landing Page** | Establish relevance, trust, and a low-pressure first step. | Thai value proposition, four-step model, sample future possibilities, privacy and non-prediction message. | Slim glass header, split hero, generated illustration, mint CTA, three bento process cards, source/safety footer. | **เริ่มค้นหาแบบ Guest** | Preview a result, switch theme, or start discovery; no account wall. | Illustration becomes a compact top card; headline and CTA remain above the fold; four-tab bottom navigation. | 56/44 split hero, bento grid below, persistent trust note and start action. | Semantic landmarks, descriptive illustration alt, visible focus, 16px+ Thai body, static fallback for motion. |
| **2. Sign Up and Login** | Save progress securely only when needed. | Guest benefit, phone/OTP and email, role, consent summary, recovery. | Authentication card, show/hide fields, OTP state, age-aware consent, error summary, privacy link. | **บันทึกความคืบหน้า** | Passwordless sign-in; retain the current journey after authentication. | Single column, correct input modes, keyboard-safe CTA, no forced social login. | Centered card plus an explanation of what will and will not be stored. | Proper labels/autocomplete, errors linked with `aria-describedby`, no time-limited OTP without extension. |
| **3. User Onboarding** | Collect the minimum context needed to adapt questions. | Education level, decision horizon, location/access constraints, input preference, accessibility, consent. | Step indicator, large option cards, “ยังไม่แน่ใจ,” save/exit, data-use summary. | **เริ่มคุยกับ Future Me** | Progressive disclosure; every answer can be skipped or edited. | One question per screen; thumb-reachable options; retains draft offline. | Two-column prompt and contextual explanation; no long form. | Fieldset/legend for options, plain Thai, no color-only selection, focus moves to step heading. |
| **4. AI Interview Page** | Gather real behavioral evidence through a safe adaptive conversation. | One Socratic/STAR question, recent messages, reflective summary, evidence and gaps. | Progress, coach identity/boundary, chat, quick replies, text/voice input, editable evidence drawer, pause/delete, human handoff. | **ส่งคำตอบ / ยืนยันสิ่งที่เข้าใจ** | Answer, request example, reject/edit inference, pause, or ask for counselor support. | Evidence drawer becomes a bottom sheet; composer stays above keyboard; messages never require swipe. | 2:1 conversation/evidence layout with calm prompt focus. | Message author labels, live announcements, keyboard controls, transcript controls, no typing animation under reduced motion. |
| **5. Personality and Interest Assessment** | Add consistent RIASEC signals without turning them into a fixed identity. | 30 statements in short groups, response scale, examples, limitations. | Progress, statement card, labeled scale, back/review, “ไม่แน่ใจ,” text result alternative. | **บันทึกสัญญาณความสนใจ** | Rate statements, review answers, learn why a question matters. | One item or small group; persistent progress; no tiny radar-only result. | Question plus context and progress; optional keyboard shortcuts with visible help. | Radio semantics, complete labels, no forced timer, chart duplicated as text list. |
| **6. Skills and Strengths Analysis** | Separate demonstrated strengths, emerging skills, and hypotheses. | Evidence from stories/missions, transferable skills, energy, gaps, constraints. | Evidence cards, skill clusters, source/status chips, list/radar toggle, challenge/edit. | **เลือกทักษะที่จะลองต่อ** | Open source evidence, correct a label, add an example, select a mission. | Default to sorted list; visualization is optional and horizontally safe. | Bento analysis: proven, emerging, unknown, and next experiment. | Status uses text/icon; source links have context; chart has table/list equivalent. |
| **7. Career Recommendation Results** | Present three explainable route strategies and trade-offs. | Balanced, Interest Growth, Practical Access; activities, evidence, unknowns, constraints, next experiments. | Three route cards, strategy labels, comparison drawer, source freshness, evidence link, disclaimer. | **เทียบ 3 เส้นทาง** | Save, compare, challenge, expand evidence, or start a differentiating mission. | Cards stack in equal priority; sticky compare tray; no swipe-only carousel. | Three-column glass bento with a shared comparison bar. | No rank/percentage match; headings identify cards; all expandable content keyboard accessible. |
| **8. Education Path Recommendation** | Connect routes to realistic Thai learning options. | Upper secondary, vocational/DVE, higher education/TCAS, course, time/cost, prerequisites, official sources. | Path cards, eligibility/freshness labels, timeline, cost and access questions, counselor prompt. | **เพิ่มตัวเลือกไว้เปรียบเทียบ** | Filter by constraints, inspect source date, compare prerequisites, flag incorrect info. | Filters open in a labeled sheet; source and verification warning stay visible. | Table/card hybrid with sticky comparison and source column. | Tables reflow to labeled cards; abbreviations expanded; stale data never encoded only in color. |
| **9. Personal Future Roadmap** | Turn a route hypothesis into an editable plan with prerequisites. | Current state, 30-day mission, study branches, portfolio, experience, review checkpoints. | Timeline nodes, dependencies, branch labels, list/DAG toggle, check-in, future-self card. | **เริ่มการทดลอง 30 วัน** | Complete, reschedule, branch, reflect, or change the goal without losing history. | Vertical list is default; branches stack and retain labels; CTA remains reachable. | Center timeline with side evidence and conditional future-self preview. | DOM order matches chronology; list mode complete; status not color-only; motion optional. |
| **10. User Dashboard** | Make the next useful action obvious and preserve continuity. | Next mission, evidence coverage, saved routes, roadmap, sharing status, recent changes. | Hero mission card, evidence list/radar, saved paths, 30-day checklist, privacy card. | **ทำภารกิจถัดไป** | Resume, check a task, inspect what changed, manage sharing. | Single-column priority order; compact bottom tabs; no dense social feed. | Asymmetric bento grid with mission as largest tile. | Heading order follows DOM rather than visual grid; chart text alternative; controls 44px+. |
| **11. Saved Careers or Programs** | Organize options without implying commitment. | Saved careers, programs, courses, notes, source dates, comparison sets. | Search/filter, compare checkbox, freshness label, notes, archive/remove. | **เปรียบเทียบตัวเลือกที่เลือก** | Group, annotate, remove, export questions for counselor. | Filter sheet and stacked cards; selection count is announced. | Sortable table/card hybrid with side comparison tray. | Accessible checkbox names; sortable headers announce order; destructive removal confirms target. |
| **12. User Profile** | Give the learner ownership of context, preferences, and data. | Education context, assessment versions, evidence history, theme/accessibility, notifications, export/delete, consent. | Editable sections, privacy center, access log, security, data controls. | **ตรวจและแก้ข้อมูลของฉัน** | Edit, export, withdraw consent, revoke links, delete selected or all data. | Separate settings pages; destructive actions not near routine toggles. | Two-column settings navigation and content panel. | Clear consequences, confirmation, status feedback, accessible toggles, no dark patterns. |
| **13. Progress Tracking** | Show learning over time without public or streak pressure. | Completed missions, evidence gained, route changes, reflections, future checkpoint. | Private timeline, milestone cards, change log, pause, celebrate/reflection prompt. | **ทบทวนสิ่งที่ได้เรียนรู้** | Add reflection, compare earlier assumptions, pause reminders, update roadmap. | Vertical timeline; milestone summaries collapsed with clear controls. | Timeline plus evidence-change panel and roadmap impact. | No shame copy, rank, streak, or peer comparison; expanded state and dates announced. |
| **14. Parent or Teacher Summary** | Support a constructive human conversation while protecting private details. | Student-selected strengths, three routes, questions, 30-day plan, source notes, limitations. | Scope/recipient/expiry, transcript-excluded notice, preview, print/PDF, revoke, request conversation. | **แชร์สรุปแบบจำกัดสิทธิ์** | Student previews fields, grants time-limited access, views access log, and revokes. | Readable report cards; sharing controls precede send; print remains optional. | Print-friendly light layout with consent banner and discussion prompts. | Raw transcript off by default; role/purpose clear; print contrast; links and expiry have plain-text meaning. |

## Prototype scope

The responsive prototype implements Landing, AI Interview, Career Results, Dashboard, and Future Roadmap. Authentication, onboarding, assessment, skills, education paths, saved items, profile, progress, and the parent/teacher summary are fully specified here for future product work.
