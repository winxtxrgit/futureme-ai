# The Mirror Interview — บทสนทนากับตัวเอง

## Concept snapshot

| Item | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Concept name** | The Mirror Interview / บทสนทนากับตัวเอง |
| **One-sentence description** | A reflective interview lets students recognize patterns in their own stories before seeing any career label. |
| **Main idea** | A private reflection studio for students who dislike conventional personality quizzes. |
| **Target user group** | Introspective Thai students in ม.4–university year 1 who want language for what matters to them. |
| **Main user problem** | Multiple-choice tests flatten identity and encourage socially desirable answers. |
| **Unique value proposition** | See the evidence behind each interest signal as it emerges from your own stories. |
| **Emotional experience** | Safe, thoughtful, validated, and pleasantly surprised. |
| **Visual direction** | Frosted glass, editorial collage, soft violet gradients, mirror facets, and quiet gallery pacing. |
| **Development difficulty** | Medium–High |
| **Recommended technology** | Next.js state machine, streaming LLM responses, structured evidence store, RIASEC rule engine, and encrypted journal data. |

## Main features

- Reflective chapters
- Editable AI summaries
- RIASEC facet view
- Values ladder
- Evidence journal
- Route hypotheses
- Private share packet

## Advantages

- Strong differentiation from static tests
- Builds self-awareness
- Excellent trust and consent moments

## Disadvantages

- Longer session
- Requires strong summarization UX and careful language

## Why this fits Future Me

It closely follows Socratic questioning, motivational interviewing, laddering, and the requirement to avoid premature labels.

## Product positioning

**Category:** Interactive personality interview  
**Promise:** See the evidence behind each interest signal as it emerges from your own stories.  
**Primary CTA:** เปิดห้องสะท้อนตัวตน  
**Voice:** Reflective, non-judgmental, precise, and spacious.

## Distinct interaction model

Chaptered reflective prompts, pause-to-note moments, and editable evidence fragments.

### Navigation structure

Minimal centered navigation: Reflect, Evidence, Possibilities, Journal.

- Desktop: Floating centered pill navigation that disappears during focused interview mode.
- Mobile: A single progress rail and back control; navigation returns after each reflection chapter.

### Core layouts

- **Homepage:** Gallery-like split hero with a luminous mirror visual and floating reflection cards.
- **AI interview:** Full-screen reflection chapters with a live mirror of summarized phrases—not a chat transcript.
- **Dashboard:** A private journal arranged by themes, evidence moments, and unresolved questions.
- **Results:** Six interest facets unfold into route families; users can challenge or edit each inference.
- **Roadmap:** A journal timeline connecting reflections, trials, and decisions.

## Design guardrails

- Present routes as hypotheses for educational exploration, never guaranteed predictions.
- Keep private chat content separate from any parent/teacher summary.
- Keep official admissions facts, source dates, and assumptions distinguishable.
- Offer a list alternative to any graph, map, or highly visual interaction.
- Make “ยังไม่รู้” and “แก้ไขสิ่งที่ AI เข้าใจ” first-class actions.
