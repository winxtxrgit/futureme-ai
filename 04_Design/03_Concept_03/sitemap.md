# Timefold Sitemap

## Navigation model

Timeline-first navigation: Now, Try, Study, Build, Work.

```text
Public
├── Home — Immersive dark hero with the generated timeline as a cinematic background and a restrained copy panel.
├── How Timefold works
├── Sample experience
├── For students
├── For families and schools
├── Privacy, safety, evidence
└── Sign in

Authenticated student
├── Timeline-first navigation: Now, Try, Study, Build, Work.
├── Discovery
│   ├── Onboarding
│   ├── AI interview
│   ├── RIASEC interest signal
│   ├── Scenario mission
│   └── Evidence review
├── Possibilities
│   ├── Three-route result
│   ├── Career detail
│   ├── Education-path detail
│   └── Compare and sources
├── Personal roadmap
├── Saved items
├── Progress
├── Share summary
└── Profile and privacy
```

## Responsive navigation

- Desktop: A horizontal chapter rail with the current stage pinned.
- Mobile: A thumb-friendly scrubber for moving between timeline chapters.
- Focus mode: Interview and assessment views suppress nonessential navigation but retain Exit, Save, Help, and Privacy.
